Management System Certification

Auditing and certifying the management systems of organizations in an impartial, objective and independent manner.

Focused on Asia, accepting applications worldwide Voluntary certification, not yet accredited by an accreditation body

Scope of certification

A certificate indicates only that the certified client’s management system within the scope of the certificate conforms to the applicable certification requirements; it does not constitute an assurance of conformity of its products, services, process outcomes or individual activities. Certificates do not bear any accreditation mark.

Certification process

  1. 01ApplicationProvide the organization’s name, contact information, the standards and scope for which certification is sought, sites, countries or regions of operation, main activities, and products or services.
  2. 02Application review and contract reviewConfirmation that the information is complete, that the necessary competence and resources are available, and that there are no unacceptable impartiality, confidentiality or other risks.
  3. 03Stage 1 auditConfirmation of the management system’s readiness, scope, sites, processes, external requirements, documentation, internal audit and management review.
  4. 04Stage 2 auditEvaluation of the implementation and effectiveness of the management system, obtaining objective evidence sufficient to support the certification decision.
  5. 05Certification decisionAfter review of the audit results and the handling of nonconformities, a decision is made to grant or refuse certification.
  6. 06Certificate issuanceCertificate numbers follow the format MSCB-scheme code-year-serial number, e.g. MSCB-ISMS-2026-001.
  7. 07Surveillance and recertification auditsThe certification cycle is 3 years, counted from the certification decision. Surveillance audits are conducted in years 1 and 2, at least once each calendar year; a recertification audit is conducted in year 3, before the certificate expires.

Certification status

Certification status changes in accordance with certification decisions and is updated accordingly in Certificate Search.

Valid

Granted, maintained or renewed: the management system continues to conform to requirements and remains effective.

Suspended

Persistent or serious failure to meet requirements, failure to allow surveillance audits, misuse of the certificate or mark, or similar; during suspension, the certification may not be claimed as valid.

Withdrawn

The cause of suspension is not resolved within the set time frame, there is a serious breach of requirements, or the validity of certification cannot be maintained.

Expired

The certificate’s period of validity ends without recertification having been completed.

Appeals and complaints

Appeals

Submitted by
Applicant organization, client or certified client
Subject
A certification decision relating to the submitter
Acknowledgement
Always acknowledged
Handled by
Personnel other than those who carried out the original audit and made the original decision
Notification
Progress reports are provided; the outcome is formally notified on completion

Complaints

Submitted by
Any person or organization
Subject
Certification activities, certified clients, use of marks, impartiality, confidentiality or service quality
Acknowledgement
Acknowledged where practicable, with confirmation of whether the matter is AISML’s responsibility
Handled by
Personnel not involved in the subject of the complaint
Notification
Progress reports are provided where practicable; formal notice is given on completion
