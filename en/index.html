What we do

Researching international standards

We study ISO and other international standards, focusing on management systems for artificial intelligence, information security, privacy information and compliance. Our findings are published as technical reports.

Technical reports

Connecting public resources

We compile public resources such as government R&D programs, incentives and subsidies, and academic research, help organizations in need find applicable resources, and foster collaboration among industry, academia and research institutions.

About us

Management system certification

Our management system certification body audits and certifies the management systems of organizations in an impartial, objective and independent manner.

Learn about the certification process

One cycle, continuous verification.

Certification cycle

3years

Counted from the certification decision; a recertification audit is conducted in year 3, before the certificate expires.

Surveillance audit

≥1/ year

Conducted at least once each calendar year, except in the recertification year.

Initial certification

2stages

Stage 1 confirms readiness; stage 2 evaluates implementation and effectiveness.
