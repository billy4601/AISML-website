Privacy Notice

How this website handles your information.

1. The website itself does not collect personal data

This website is a static website. It has no member accounts, online forms or databases, does not use cookies, website analytics or tracking tools, and does not load third-party advertising or social media plug-ins.

Searching and filtering in Certificate Search are performed in your browser, and your queries are not sent to AISML. Filter conditions are shown in the URL so that results can be shared; when a URL containing filter conditions is opened, the URL is sent to the website host, as with any website.

2. Website host logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. According to GitHub, GitHub logs the IP addresses of visitors for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor is signed in to GitHub. These logs are processed and retained by GitHub in accordance with its Privacy Statement. AISML does not obtain, and does not separately retain, website connection logs.

3. Personal data provided by email

When you email Service@aisml.org, you provide personal data such as your name, contact details and the content of your message. Pursuant to Article 8 of the Personal Data Protection Act (Taiwan), we hereby notify you as follows:

  • Collector: Ming Chi University of Technology (carried out by the AI Security Management Lab (AISML), Department of Electrical Engineering).
  • Purposes of collection: To respond to information requests and to handle inquiries about certification applications, appeals and complaints.
  • Categories of personal data: Your name and contact details such as email address and telephone number, and the content you provide in your message.
  • Period, area, recipients and method of use: Retained for 5 years from the date the message is received and deleted when that period ends; during this period, used by AISML personnel by email or in writing within the territory of the Republic of China (Taiwan). Not provided to any third party unless otherwise provided by law or with your consent.
  • Your rights: You may request Ming Chi University of Technology to allow you to inquire into and review your personal data, to provide copies of it, to supplement or correct it, to cease collecting, processing or using it, and to delete it.
  • Effect of not providing data: Providing your personal data is voluntary. If you do not provide the necessary contact information, AISML may be unable to respond to or handle your request.

The identity of persons submitting appeals and complaints, and the content of their submissions, are handled in accordance with the confidentiality commitments in the AISML Management System Certification Body Public Statement.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this notice or your personal data, please email Service@aisml.org. You may also refer to the Ming Chi University of Technology Personal Data Protection Management Policy Statement or contact its personal data protection contact point.
