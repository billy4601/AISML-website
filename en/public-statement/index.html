Management System Certification Body Public Statement

Document No. MSCB-A-02 | Version 260628.0.1 | Effective date 2026-02-02

← Public Documents

1. Scope

1.1 This Statement sets out the public commitments of AISML, acting as a management system certification body, regarding impartiality, confidentiality, public information, certification status, the use of certificates and marks, and the handling of appeals and complaints.

1.2 This Statement applies to the information on management system certification activities that AISML discloses publicly, including AIMS, ISMS, PIMS, CMS and other management system certification schemes approved by AISML.

2. Normative references

2.1 This Statement refers to the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015 concerning principles, general requirements, information requirements, and the handling of appeals and complaints.

Note: The corresponding National Standard of the Republic of China (Taiwan) is CNS 17021-1:2019, Conformity assessment — Requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of management systems — Part 1: Requirements.

3. Terms and definitions

3.1 This Statement uses terms commonly used in the field of management system certification. Any questions about terminology, certification services or the application of this Statement may be addressed to AISML.

4. Impartiality statement

4.1 AISML is committed to having competent personnel carry out management system certification activities in an impartial, objective and independent manner, and is responsible for the certification decisions it makes.

4.2 Neither AISML, nor any part of the same legal entity, nor any entity under the organizational control of AISML, shall offer or provide management system consultancy, or offer, provide or assist in carrying out internal audits of the management systems of applicant organizations or certified clients. AISML does not link, bundle or confuse certification activities with consultancy activities, does not circumvent the prohibition on internal audits by describing them as inspections, assessments, reviews or by any other name, and does not imply that the use of any particular consultancy, training or service would lead to a more favorable certification outcome.

4.3 AISML identifies, evaluates and addresses risks that may affect impartiality, including those relating to ownership, governance, management, finances, business relationships, external personnel, client relationships and other conflicts of interest.

4.4 AISML oversees impartiality risks through an impartiality committee or an equivalent mechanism. Anyone who becomes aware of a situation that may affect the impartiality of AISML’s certification activities may submit information or a complaint to AISML.

5. Confidentiality statement

5.1 AISML is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of client information, personal data, technical data, information security data, privacy information, artificial intelligence system information, compliance incidents and other confidential information obtained in the course of certification activities.

5.2 AISML does not disclose client confidential information to outside parties except as required by law, by an accreditation body or by a competent authority, as permitted by contract, or as authorized by the client. Where information is disclosed as required by law or by a competent authority, AISML will notify the client unless prohibited by law.

6. Certification information and certification status

6.1 AISML provides, as required, public information on the scope of its management system certification services, certification requirements, application procedures, the handling of appeals and complaints, and the requirements for the use of certificates and marks.

6.2 Interested parties may inquire with AISML about the certification status of a specific certified client. AISML may, in accordance with applicable requirements, provide the certified client’s name, certification scope, applicable standards, certificate status and other necessary public information.

6.3 A management system certificate issued by AISML indicates only that the certified client’s management system within the scope of the certificate conforms to the applicable certification requirements. It does not constitute an assurance by AISML of product conformity in respect of the certified client’s products, services, process outcomes or individual activities.

7. Use of certificates and marks

7.1 Certified clients shall use certificates, marks and references to certification in accordance with AISML’s rules and applicable accreditation requirements, and shall not imply in a misleading manner that any scope, product, service or site not covered has been certified.

7.2 Where AISML finds misuse of certificates, marks or references to certification, it may require the certified client to correct it, cease such use or issue a public correction, or may, in accordance with its rules, suspend certification, withdraw certification or take other necessary measures.

8. Appeals and complaints

8.1 Any applicant organization, client or certified client that disagrees with a certification decision made by AISML relating to it may submit an appeal to AISML.

8.2 Any person or organization dissatisfied with AISML’s certification activities, a certified client’s management system, the use of certificates or marks, impartiality, confidentiality or service quality may submit a complaint to AISML.

8.3 AISML will handle appeals and complaints in an impartial, confidential and traceable manner. Personnel involved in handling an appeal will be different from those who carried out the original audit and made the original certification decision. The decision to be communicated to the appellant or complainant will be made, or reviewed and approved, by persons not previously involved in the subject of the appeal or complaint.

8.4 Email for appeals and complaints: Service@aisml.org

9. Maintenance of this Statement

9.1 AISML will keep this Statement consistent with the certification services it actually provides and with applicable requirements. When certification services, public information, appeal and complaint procedures, rules for the use of certificates and marks, or applicable requirements change, AISML will evaluate this Statement and update it as appropriate.
