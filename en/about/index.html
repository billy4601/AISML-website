AISML

AI Security
Management Lab

A university research laboratory in the Department of Electrical Engineering, Ming Chi University of Technology, led by Assistant Professor En-Cheng Liou and run by Principal Researcher Bo-Yang Lu.

Researching international standards

We study ISO and other international standards, focusing on management systems for artificial intelligence, information security, privacy information and compliance, and examine their requirements, conformity assessment and impact on organizations.

Connecting public resources

We compile public resources such as government R&D programs, incentives and subsidies, and academic research, help organizations in need find applicable resources, and foster collaboration among industry, academia and research institutions.

Management system certification

Our management system certification body provides management system certification to ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27701 and ISO 37301.

Separation of research and certification

AISML’s research outputs and resource matching provide only public, general information. AISML does not provide any specific organization with specific advice, guidance or solutions for establishing, implementing or maintaining a management system.

Ming Chi University of Technology

Founded in 1963.
Practical education.

Located in Taishan District, New Taipei City, the university was founded by Mr. Yung-Ching Wang, founder of Formosa Plastics Group. Originally Ming Chi Junior College of Technology, it was restructured as Ming Chi Institute of Technology in 1999 and renamed Ming Chi University of Technology in 2004. It is known for practical education featuring full on-campus residence and work-study internships for all students.

Department of Electrical Engineering

A bridge between
academia and industry.

Offering undergraduate and master’s programs, the department combines artificial intelligence, communications, networking and conventional electrical engineering technologies, with key areas of development including electric power, intelligent control and communications.
